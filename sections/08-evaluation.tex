\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

We answer five questions:
\textbf{Q1} How strong are \A1--\A6 under realistic load (\cref{sec:eval-attacks})?
\textbf{Q2} Does \sys close them, and how do existing defenses compare (\cref{sec:eval-defense})?
\textbf{Q3} What capacity does \sys keep relative to global sharing and isolation (\cref{sec:eval-capacity})?
\textbf{Q4} How sensitive is it to quotas, sharing structure, bandwidth and $\Lpub$ (\cref{sec:eval-sensitivity})?
\textbf{Q5} What does it cost (\cref{sec:eval-overhead})?

\subsection{Setup}
\paragraph{Hardware and models.} \tbd{Nodes, GPUs, NICs.} Models:
\tbd{Llama-3.1-8B, a 14B--32B Qwen model, and a 70B-class model (or dummy
weights at 70B shape, as \mc's own reproducible evaluation used~\cite{qin2025mooncake})},
plus \tbd{one MLA model} to test how smaller per-token KV changes channel strength.

\paragraph{Workloads.} \mc's released conversation and tool\&agent
traces~\cite{qin2025mooncake}, and its synthetic mix of ShareGPT, L-Eval and
LooGLE in a 1:1:1 ratio. The traces carry remapped block hashes but no
principal IDs; we assign principals by \tbd{session clustering on shared
prefix chains} and sweep the number of principals and the share of
cross-principal reuse. \todo{Validate the assignment method; it drives every
capacity number.}

\paragraph{Baselines.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Global}: unrestricted \mc sharing (insecure performance ceiling).
\item \textbf{Salt}: per-principal \texttt{cache\_salt}~\cite{vllmcachesalt}.
\item \textbf{KVGov}: salt at the divergence boundary~\cite{addagada2026kvgov}.
\item \textbf{CacheSolidarity}: detect-and-isolate~\cite{pennas2026cachesolidarity},
  ported from its released vLLM artifact.
\item \textbf{SafeKV}: sensitivity-based selective sharing~\cite{chu2025safekv}.
\item \textbf{Pad}: constant TTFT for all requests.
\item \textbf{\sys} and ablations: no slack reclamation; admission on $\pp$; no
  accounting scrubber; no pinning.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Metrics.} Security: per-channel AUC, attack success rate, queries per
recovered token, deadline-miss rate. Performance: \mc's effective request
capacity (fraction of requests meeting both TTFT and TBT
SLOs)~\cite{qin2025mooncake}, prefill GPU-seconds per request, physical hit
rate, TTFT p50/p99, TBT p99.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Cluster-scale KV caches turn prefix reuse from a node optimization into shared
infrastructure, and with it into a shared leak that reaches beyond timing to
admission, billing and eviction. \sys shows that the fix need not give up the
sharing: by deciding what each principal may observe from a private,
counterfactual cache while the physical cache stays global, it makes a
\mc-style system look isolated to its clients and shared to its operator.
